Model notes
  • Explicit 7-point finite-difference scheme (Eqs. 3-4) on cuboid voxel elements Δx × Δy × Δz, deposited in a zigzag: each layer starts at x = 0 on strand j = 1 (front, y = 0), strands alternate direction, every layer repeats the same path. No travel time.
  • Missing neighbours (not yet deposited, or outside the part) use the convection + radiation boundary of Eqs. 6-13 with the selection rules of Fig. 13. The O(Δ²) form is used when the opposite neighbour exists, otherwise the O(Δ) form. Convection uses Ta(z), radiation uses T∞ (footnote 3).
  • Fig. 13 is followed literally, including the single-wall case (W = one strand), where the left side takes the O(Δy²) form built on the right-side boundary value.
  • Known issue kept on purpose: the O(Δ²) boundary form doubles the exchange with the opposite neighbour, so heat is not conserved where it is used (for example the top element of each layer). This reproduces the results of the original T4F3 model; it is not a physically conservative scheme.
  • Checked against the paper (Set 2, constant ρ = 1.226 g/cm³, cp = 1.801 J/g/K): deposition times 141.30 / 138.60 / 34.65 s match, h = 60 vs 8.5 gives a 26 °C max difference (paper 26 °C), ε = 0.78 vs 0 gives 3.4 °C (paper 4.1 °C, with dynamic properties), and v = 60 mm/s gives ~157 °C at the inter-layer reheat (paper ~160 °C).
  • Storage: probes chosen before a run are recorded at every time step. The full temperature field is stored at every step when it fits in about 160 MB, otherwise every n-th step (shown after the run). Probes added after a run use the stored field.
  • Not implemented: wide flat nozzle tip (no equations in the paper).
